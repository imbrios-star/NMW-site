#### Description

This standalone app focuses on five-character verse and tonal statistics. It examines how inputted verse conforms to the conjectural Yongming Triad rule. The analysis is inspired by, but differs from, the work of Zhang Hongming, "On the Origin of Chinese Tonal Prosody: Argumentation from a Case Study of Shen Yue's Poems," Journal of Chinese Literature and Culture (2015) 2 (2): 347–379.
