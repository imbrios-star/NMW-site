Middle Chinese Tone Toolkit

Open any of the HTML files directly in a browser. They are standalone and already
include the current tone database.

Files:
- middle-chinese-tone-identifier-targeted.html
  Identifies Middle Chinese tone and compact Ping-Ze output for pasted text.

- poem-tone-analyzer.html
  Analyzes four-, five-, and seven-character verse, with line/couplet statistics
  and line-specific ambiguity overrides.

- pentasyllabic-tone-analyzer.html
  Specialized five-character verse analyzer with Zhang-style tonal statistics.

- middle-chinese-tone-data-editor-targeted.xlsx
  Editable tone database workbook.

- middle-chinese-tone-data-editor-targeted.csv
  Plain-text backup/source version of the tone database.

After editing the workbook or CSV, the HTML apps need to be regenerated before
they reflect those changes.
